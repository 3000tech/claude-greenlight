<objective>
Finish the hook side of the flip and close FLIP-02: make the turn-end rule badge-only at the source, retire the AskUserQuestion lock hook, and bring the test suites to equivalent coverage on state-file fixtures.

Purpose: the monitor now renders hook-derived state, but `hooks/state-writer.sh` still encodes the pre-flip rule that a running background task holds a session at working — the exact rule 02-DIVERGENCE-REVIEW carry-forward #2 says flips to badge-only, and the source of divergence class 3's 37 events. Retiring the AskUserQuestion lock (D-04) removes one of the two corrective-patch hooks the whole refactor was framed around. FLIP-02 then locks in what all of this actually produced.

Output: badge-only turn-end semantics at the hook; an installer that ships the retired-hook teardown alongside a default path that no longer installs it; a validated working-lock; and three green suites with no user-facing guarantee left uncovered.
</objective>
